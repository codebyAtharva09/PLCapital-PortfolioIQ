## 7. Manual review notes

- **Home Depot (HD) is classified as Consumer Staples** in the supplied data, while common industry classification places it in Consumer Discretionary. The supplied assignment is preserved and flagged as a source-data classification anomaly wherever HD affects an answer (all of International Equity Fund's Consumer Staples exposure is HD).
- **`status` holds `Active` / `Passive`**, which reads as management style rather than lifecycle status. "Active portfolios" is taken literally as `status = 'Active'`.
- **`market_cap` units are not documented.** Values are consistent with USD millions (AAPL = 2,800,000), but answers do not assume a unit.
- **Two bonds mature after the data's as-of date but before today** (UST-2Y on 2024-01-15, UST-5Y on 2025-03-01). Answers are framed at the data's as-of date, not the current date.
